Excuse me, I am calling on behalf of my neighborhood
to report a theft of candy that occurred this Halloween.
You see, every year each household in my neighborhood
leaves a bucket of sweets and other items with a sign
informing each person can only take one item of each type.

Unfortunately, it appears that a nefarious criminal
took one more sweet treat from a household than they
should have. Luckily, we require every trick-or-treater
to report the exact amount of items they have taken,
which I hope will be useful to find the culprit of
this dastardly deed.
